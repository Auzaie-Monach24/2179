Malaysia 2025 Housing Affordability and Labour Market Conditions

This dashboard tells a state-by-state story about whether expensive and active housing markets align with stronger or weaker labour conditions. Housing records describe township prices, tenure, property type and transaction activity, while the quarterly labour dataset adds labour force size, employment, unemployment and participation rate for 2025.

By: Auzaie Nasir | FIT2179 | DATA VISUALIZATION II

Where are Malaysia's higher-priced housing markets?

The map starts the dashboard geographically by showing how weighted median property prices vary across states.

Darker states point to more expensive housing markets, helping the reader quickly locate where affordability pressure is likely to be higher before moving into labour comparisons.

Housing price and labour context

Do higher-price states also face weaker labour conditions?

This scatterplot connects the two datasets using State/state. Median_Price is compared against u_rate, while bubble size shows Transactions.

The chart helps identify whether expensive markets are supported by healthier labour conditions or whether some states combine high prices with higher unemployment risk.

Which states recorded the strongest housing activity?

Transaction totals reveal where market activity is concentrated, not just where prices are highest.

The colour channel adds Median_PSF, making it possible to separate high-volume affordable markets from high-volume premium markets.

Market structure

How does property type mix differ across active states?

The normalised stacked bar focuses on composition rather than raw totals. This shows whether each active state is driven more by terrace houses, apartments, condominiums or other property types.

This matters because two states can have similar transaction totals but very different housing-market structures.

Which property type dominates the national market?

The donut chart gives a compact national summary of Transactions by Type.

It works as a quick part-to-whole view before the dashboard drills down into state and township patterns.

Which state type combinations are the most active?

The heatmap reveals dense combinations of State and Type using Transactions.

The Tenure filter adds ownership context, allowing the reader to test whether activity patterns change when focusing on Freehold, Leasehold or other tenure categories.

Quarterly labour movement

Do employment and unemployment move together during 2025?

This dual-axis line chart uses the actual labour columns: lf_employed on the left scale and lf_unemployed on the right scale.

The state filter supports a focused reading of quarterly labour movement without flattening unemployment under the much larger employed-person count.

Which states improved or worsened in unemployment?

The dumbbell chart compares u_rate between the early and later 2025 quarters.

A longer connecting line indicates a larger labour-market shift, making it easier to spot states where unemployment changed noticeably during the year.

Advanced market profile and local hotspots

Which states combine high activity with high price levels?

The radial profile compresses two housing indicators into one advanced idiom: angle shows Transactions, while radius represents Median_Price.

This gives a quick profile of states that are both active and expensive, compared with markets that are active but more moderate in price.

Where are the township transaction hotspots?

The lollipop chart drills down from state-level patterns into Township and Area records.

State and Type filters reduce clutter, so the reader can compare the most active localities for a selected property category instead of viewing every township at once.
